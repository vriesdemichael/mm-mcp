    Views¶

    Some AI apps can show more than text: small, interactive panels inside the chat, which MCP calls views. mm-mcp uses views to show you Mattermost as Mattermost shows it. You see a thread you asked about, what you missed, and every post your agent makes. A post your agent drafts for you waits there for you to check, change and send yourself.

    Everything below is live. Each view is the same one mm-mcp shows in your AI app, playing back a recorded conversation from a test Mattermost. Press anything: nothing is sent from this page, and Start over puts a view back as it was.

    Does my app show views, and what do I need?

    VS Code shows them once chat.mcp.apps.enabled is on in its settings. Claude Code shows them in the Claude desktop app, to read. Ask your agent to run diagnose and it tells you whether yours does (Installation).

    Seeing a thread, or what you missed, works as soon as mm-mcp is set up. Drafting a post, and Post, Edit, Discard and Mark read in a view, need writes allowed, with MM_MCP_ALLOW_WRITES=true (Configuration).

    In an app that shows no views, everything still works: your agent answers in words, and a post it drafts waits in Mattermost for you (more below).

    Check and send a post your agent drafts¶

    Ask your agent to draft something for you, and nothing is posted until you say so. The draft waits in Mattermost's message box, as your own unsent draft, and the view shows it exactly as it will look once posted.

    Try it: press Edit, change a word, save, then press Post.

    • Post sends it under your name, marked as written with AI, and clears the draft.
    • Edit changes the draft. Once you have changed it, it is yours: your agent will not replace it.
    • Discard deletes the draft.
    • A link shows where it really goes, next to the words it is written as.

    If you change the draft in Mattermost while the view is open, the view notices, and leaves the draft to you there instead of sending the old text. If your agent proposes again, your next press shows you the new text first.

    A message to the whole channel¶

    Mattermost asks before a message with @channel, @all or @here notifies more than five people. The view asks too, with the number of people it will notify, counted as Mattermost counts them.

    Try it: press Post, then Cancel or Post anyway.

    When you already started a message there¶

    Mattermost keeps one unsent draft in each channel and in each thread. If yours is already there, your agent never writes over it:

    • You asked your agent to improve it: the view shows the suggested changes, word by word, with Apply and Keep mine.
    • Otherwise: the view shows your draft next to the suggestion, with Replace mine and Keep mine.

    Nothing is changed until you press.

    See a thread¶

    Ask to see a thread and your agent shows it with show. You get pictures, formatting, reactions, custom emoji, images that open full size in their post, and the cards a webhook or bot posts, such as an alert with its red or green bar, and the blocks an integration posts on Mattermost 11.11 and later, with their columns and the sections you unfold. A link to another post shows that post quoted below it, a long one cut short with Show more for the rest: press the quote to read the post, in the view where your app allows it and otherwise in Mattermost. A link to a web page shows the page's card. A picture a link or a card leads to is named, and opens in your browser: the view itself reaches nothing outside Mattermost. A post marked Important or Urgent carries its label, and one that asks to be acknowledged says how many did, with who when you point at it. A burn-on-read message you have not opened stays concealed, as in Mattermost: View message in Mattermost takes you there to open it. A call shows when it started and by whom, or when it ended, how long it lasted and who was in it. A long thread loads more as you ask.

    Try it: press 2 more replies, or the chart to see it full size.

    See what you missed¶

    Ask what you missed, and the view lists:

    • your channels with unread messages, by team;
    • the posts that mention you;
    • the threads you follow that have new replies.

    Open a channel right there to read it. Where your agent may post, Mark read marks it read in Mattermost too.

    Try it: press Design to open it, then Mark read.

    See what your agent posted¶

    When your agent posts for you with create_post, dm or group_message, the post shows in the chat as it was posted, and where. An agent that posts in many places cannot do so out of your sight.

    If your app shows no views¶

    Every app still works. A post your agent drafts waits in Mattermost's message box in every Mattermost app you use, and your agent's answer links to the place where it waits. Send it, change it or clear it there.

    Which apps show views¶

    App Views Good to know
    VS Code Yes, with chat.mcp.apps.enabled on VS Code asks you before your agent drafts a post, as it does before anything that writes. Your own press in a view is not asked about again.
    Claude Code, in the Claude desktop app Yes, to read Threads and what you missed show, and on an https server a link opens after a confirmation. The button beside a view opens it in a pane of its own, where an open image fills the pane. As of Claude Code 2.1.309 it passes nothing from a view on to mm-mcp, so a thread brings its pictures along, up to 1 MiB, and only an image's preview opens large. In the other views people show as initials, and a draft waits in Mattermost for you to send, with no Post, Edit or Discard. In a terminal, Claude Code shows text.
    Codex Not checked Nobody has tried it yet. In an app that shows no views, everything still works in words.
    Goose, the desktop app Yes In its "approve" and "smart approve" modes, Goose blocks every button in a view. The view then says so and offers to open Mattermost instead.
    Claude desktop, Claude on the web Not confirmed These apps cannot be tested by machine. A check by hand is recorded on mm-mcp#37.
    An app without views No Your agent answers in words, and drafts wait in Mattermost.

    VS Code and Goose are tested with every change to mm-mcp, because a machine can drive them.

    Questions people ask¶

    Can my agent press Post for me?
    No. The buttons in a view go through a tool that only the view can use: your AI app keeps it away from the agent. Your app is trusted to do that, and to press only when you click. mm-mcp cannot tell a click from a script, so where that trust is not there, an administrator can take Post out of the view with MM_MCP_POST_FROM_VIEWS=false (Configuration). The draft then waits in Mattermost for you to send.
    Will people see that AI wrote my post?
    A post you send with Post in the view is marked as written with AI, as every post mm-mcp makes is; Mattermost shows that next to it. A draft you send from Mattermost yourself is not marked: it is yours. An administrator can turn the mark off with MM_MCP_MARK_AI_GENERATED=false.
    Can it send something different from what I see?
    No. Post sends the text the view shows, and only while your draft still holds exactly that, as the view put it there. If you typed in the draft, or attached a file to it, in Mattermost, the view leaves it to you to send from there; if your agent proposed again, the view shows you the new text instead of sending. The preview hides nothing: a link shows where it leads, a link definition Mattermost does not show is shown, and a character you cannot see, or one that turns the text's direction, is flagged, even when it is written as its number.     Does my agent know what I did?
    Your app may pass it on: the view tells it, in a fixed sentence, whether you posted, changed or discarded the draft. Not every app passes that on, so your agent can also check Mattermost: the draft gone, or the post in the channel.
    Does the view send my data anywhere?
    No. The view reaches no network. What it shows comes from mm-mcp through your app, pictures included, and mm-mcp talks to nothing but your Mattermost (Security).
    For the technically curious: what happens when you press Post 
    sequenceDiagram
    actor You
    participant App as Your AI app
    participant Model as The AI model
    participant mm as mm-mcp
    participant MM as Mattermost
    Model->>mm: propose_post, the message
    mm->>MM: save it as your draft, marked as mm-mcp's proposal
    mm-->>App: the draft
    App->>You: the view: the post as it will be posted
    You->>App: Post
    App->>mm: view_change, post, the text the view shows
    mm->>MM: does the draft still hold that text, and nothing else?
    mm->>MM: post it, marked as written with AI, and delete the draft
    mm-->>App: posted

    Technically, a view is an MCP Apps view: a page that ships inside mm-mcp, which your app runs in a sandbox without network access. Its buttons call view_change, a tool marked for views alone, which mm-mcp offers only to an app that says it shows views, and only once writes are allowed. The decisions behind this are in ADR-022 and ADR-035.
